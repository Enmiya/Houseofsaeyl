# House of Saeyl – slow cinematic background video

## Run
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `house-of-saeyl-frontend` in VS Code.
3. Open `index.html` with Live Server.

## Video changes
- Rebuilt the background montage with a locked, stable camera.
- No shake, no jitter, no zoom or parallax.
- Each supplied image holds for 7.5 seconds.
- Soft 1.6-second cinematic dissolves connect the scenes.
- Full product/craft composition stays visible within the frame.
- Warm cinematic colour grading and subtle vignette.
- Hero video remains autoplay, muted and looping.

## Page
- No separate multi-product gallery.
- The single copper-vessels client product remains in the product section.
- Brand story and WhatsApp enquiry remain.

The MP4 is an image-based cinematic montage, not footage of the objects being physically used. Confirm image licensing before public launch.
